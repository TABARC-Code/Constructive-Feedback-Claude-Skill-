# Changelog
## 1.1.0 — Socratic Method and Truthmode
---
Added a new core section, **Socratic Method: Ask Before You Answer**, positioned after the Governing Ethic and before Level Calibration — since the opening questions are what calibration now runs on. The skill asks one or two questions before explaining, diagnosing, or giving feedback, aimed at locating the student's existing understanding and their actual goal, rather than answering the literal surface question.


Introduced **Truthmode** as the named tone for this questioning: honesty carried by warmth rather than softened by it. A question should never feel like a trap; an honest answer should never feel cold. Wrong guesses are corrected plainly, not left uncorrected in the name of encouragement.


I applied the reciprocal edit to `subskills/constructive-feedback/constructive-feedback.md`, per standing convention — submitted-work sessions now open with the same Socratic questions ("What were you trying to argue?", "Which part are you least confident about?") before the existing Step 0 logistics and feedback-mode selection. Cross-referenced the existing "Ask Useful Questions" step (feedback sequence step 6) back to the parent's Socratic principle, since it was already doing the same thing mid-diagnosis without naming it.

Updated the frontmatter description to surface the Socratic method and Truthmode tone for triggering purposes. Added `version` fields to both the parent skill and the sub-skill for consistency with the rest of the skill repository.

Added this file, along with `README.md` and `DESCRIPTION.md`, none of which previously existed for this skill.

It just work better.

## 1.0.0 — Initial build

Established the core tutor: Master's-level subject identity, with  the governing ethic (teach, don't flatter, don't secretly complete the work - (This is an importaant distinction)),  level calibration, discipline-specific awareness across six field groups, honesty and uncertainty standards, intellectual humility, source awareness, layered explanation and misunderstanding-handling sequences, learner-type adaptation, motivation handling, academic integrity boundaries, and referral guidance for distress beyond the scope of a tutoring session. Established the `constructive-feedback` sub-skill for submitted-work review, with its own feedback-mode selection, nine-step feedback sequence, bluntness rules, and submission-type checklists.
